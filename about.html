Who we serve: Students, entrepreneurs, traders, corporate clients, and anyone looking to explore opportunities in China.

Our Story

Founded by Herbert Hawkson Nyannor, China Hub Express was born from years of experience in international trade and healthcare. After successfully establishing Americare Health LLC in the US, Herbert saw the immense potential in connecting African and Western businesses with Chinese suppliers, manufacturers, and educational institutions. What started as a one‑man consulting effort quickly grew into a full‑service agency with a passionate team. Today, we’ve helped hundreds of clients – from first‑time travelers to multinational companies – achieve their goals in China.

Our Mission
