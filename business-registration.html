The Procedure to Set Up a Company

3 Working Days

Feasibility Study Report

We provide comprehensive guidelines on company formation, legal requirements, documentation, and procedures. Our report covers Business Registration, Tax Obligations, Work Permits, and special licenses. You'll receive an accurate quotation and roadmap.

15 Working Days

Company Name Registration

After signing the contract, you can track your application online. We need five preferred company names in Chinese, plus notarized passport copies of legal representative and shareholders (or originals if you're in China). We submit to SAMR for approval.

15 Working Days

Business License (3-in-1)

Once name is approved, we draft the Articles of Association. Required: incorporation application report, overseas investor certificates notarized by Chinese Embassy, standard forms. You receive the unified Business License.

3 Working Days

Company Stamps

We engrave stamps registered with the Public Security Bureau: Company stamp, Financial stamp, Fapiao (invoice) stamp, and Legal Representative's stamp.

3 Working Days

Bank Account Opening

We assist in opening RMB company account, capital account, and foreign exchange account at a bank of your choice.

3 Working Days

Tax Registration

Within 30 days of obtaining the license, we apply for a Tax Account at the Tax Bureau, complete E-tax filing forms, and set you up for digital invoicing (E-invoice).

Basic Documents Required

Passport

If the Legal representative, Supervisor, or shareholders are foreign citizens, provide photocopies of passports. All must be notarized and authenticated by the Chinese Embassy/Consulate. If you are already in China, we can submit original passports to SAMR.

Certificate of Proof (for entity investors)

If a shareholder is a company, provide the Business Certificate notarized by the Chinese Embassy. For Hong Kong, Macau, Taiwan companies, notarization by local registration office is sufficient.

Registered Address

Chinese Company Law requires a one-year lease agreement for the registered address. Provide the Office Lease Contract and Property Certificate (copy stamped by property owner). If you don't have an office, we can help you find a registered address in a Development Zone.
